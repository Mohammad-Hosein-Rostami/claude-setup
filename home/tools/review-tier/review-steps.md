<!-- Printed by review-tier.mjs (lib/report.mjs) with every set, before its `fix round:` line: what the lead does with the set.
One bullet, printed as one line: the lead's round procedure (it runs `verdict.mjs`), then "Rules of the set", the rules of the set itself. -->
- Review round (lead): 1) save this set with `--json > tier-r<N>.json` and record its sha256; 2) launch every lens of `reviews` in one batch at its model and effort, the brief pasted as printed plus the spec, the checkpoints, the lane's P and F questions, the threat-model line and `REPORTS: <folder>`; a `skill` lens runs as a `reviewer` (sonnet, high) told "Apply the <lens> skill"; never an implementer summary or another lens's findings; 3) record each report's path and sha256 when it returns; 4) run `node ~/.claude/tools/review-tier/verdict.mjs --tier <json> --tier-sha256 <h> --report <f> --report-sha256 <h>... [--prior <f> --prior-sha256 <h>] --road <road> --road-sha256 <h> --lane <name> --write-prior <file>` with the reports in `reviews` order; 5) re-read the cited lines of each must-fix finding; drop one only with `--drop <id>` (Sonnet lens) or a judge's `ruling: <id> not-real` (`--judged`, Opus-max lens); a security finding is never dropped (`owner:` means ask the owner); 6) act on `next:`: `land` -> `land-lane`; `fix <ids>` -> one fix agent with every listed finding, its parts and siblings, then `review-tier --delta <reviewed tip> --base <base> --prior <prior-file> --prior-sha256 <printed> --lane-classes <classes>` (full shas from your own record of this round's `fix round:` line, never the writer's word); `rerun <n>` -> that lens again; `re-spec <classes>` -> the architect; `debug: <file>` -> one deep-debugger before that fix; `follow-up:` ids -> `status.mjs add` rows. A waived lane (`road.mjs join` printed `waiver-at`) runs `--waived` on `waivable.reviews`, lands with `land-lane --audit-base <base sha>`, and its `waivable.audit` pair runs in a later batch; sync and push wait for `audit-log gate`. Rules of the set: the set `review-tier` prints runs as agents or the `review` workflow (Inline, agent or workflow; `reviewer`, plus `security-auditor` and Opus lenses by class; risky, `gate` and hooks changes keep Opus max before land and every landing review keeps it, except a waived lane; reviewers keep their own loops and mutants); every fix delta goes back through `review-tier`: a lane's first review reads the whole range, never a delta, and a fix round's re-review is the delta brief of `node ~/.claude/tools/review-tier/review-tier.mjs --delta <reviewed-sha> --base <base-sha> --prior <findings-file> --prior-sha256 <its-sha256> --lane-classes <classes>` (both shas are full 40-character shas from the lead's own record of the last round, never from the writer's report and never a short sha or a name: the `fix round:` line that round printed carries them and the classes; after a rebase the base is the tip the lane was rebased onto; each findings file is one review report of that round as its reviewer wrote it, passed with its own `--prior` and `--prior-sha256` (repeat the pair for the reviewer's and the security report): the lead records each report's sha256 and finding ids the moment the reviewer returns it, the tool refuses a file whose hash differs, and a count or id it prints that differs from that record means the round is not clean; the classes are the names the `fix round:` line of the round before printed, which hold the classes a fix removed from the code), except after a waived round that found anything: its next round is the whole range with the full set; the lane's whole review set (every lens, at its model and effort, plus the lenses of any class only the fix touches, never fewer) re-checks the changed lines of the fix and what they can break until a round is clean, and a round is clean only when every reviewer's report has the line `prior: <n> fixed, 0 open` before its final line, with `n` that count; a weaker verdict never drops a stronger lens's finding (Opus judge), security findings are never dropped; `review-tier --add-class` when you see a risk the patterns miss. A tier-1 lane whose contract checkpoints passed (`waiverAt`) lands after one Sonnet lens round; a waived lane's Opus-max pair runs as the post-land audit beside the next lanes; every waived lane is audited until the owner allows sampling, and sync and push wait for `node ~/.claude/tools/review-tier/audit-log.mjs gate --repo <repo> --tip HEAD` (exit 1 stops them); an audit finding opens a `rework:` row and a fix lane, then `audit-log resolve`.
